About the conference

NGEN, the Ivy League entrepreneurship network, presents the inaugural Ivy League Entrepreneurship Conference. The mission is clear: connect world-class student entrepreneurs face-to-face with today's most influential leaders.

Formerly the Trailblazers Conference, an intimate New York gathering, the event has been reborn on a larger stage to expand its impact. Over three years, NGEN has run 18 conferences, founder treks, and pitch competitions for student founders across the Ivy League.
